AGREEMENT
Agreement contrasts deliberately rotate subjects such as Marie / Petr / děti / rodiče and therefore forms such as ptala / ptal / ptaly / ptali. Level 2 actively blanks the past-tense verb form on these records instead of leaving agreement only to the translation level.

LEVELS
1. Choose the governed phrase and its case.
2. Choose the missing form/preposition; agreement records explicitly test the past-tense verb ending.
3. Write the missing governed phrase.
4. Translate the complete sentence into Czech, including correct government and verb agreement.
5. Correct the Czech sentence; errors can involve case/preposition and, on agreement records, the past-tense ending.

DESIGN NOTE
The package deliberately does not force an arbitrary number of different government patterns onto every verb. Some Czech verbs genuinely have several productive A2-level patterns; others have one principal government pattern. For the latter, depth is added through controlled lexical/agreement practice rather than inventing unnatural constructions.
